# Cook & Bake Academy Singapore — market brief

**Positioning:** Hands-on cooking and baking courses in small classes across Orchard Road and Bukit Timah, from one-session workshops to multi-week skills training. The proposed catalogue has 20 courses, 12 places per class, and fees of S$160–S$1,580.

## Three likely customers

1. **Practical home cook:** wants repeatable kitchen skills and everyday meals; relevant courses include Knife Skills, Thai Street Food and Chinese Wok.
2. **Committed hobby baker:** wants coaching and progression through bread, sourdough, pastry and macarons.
3. **Social or gift buyer:** wants a contained experience with a take-home result; Cookie & Biscuit and Cupcake & Cake Pops are relevant.

These personas are launch hypotheses. Segment sizes are **UNKNOWN**.

## Five courses to launch first

- Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials — S$160, one session.
- Cookie & Biscuit Baking — S$180, one session.
- Thai Street Food Cooking — S$540, three sessions.
- Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking — S$680, four sessions.
- Vegetarian & Vegan Cuisine — S$540, three sessions.

## Pricing notes

Published public-class examples provide a S$120–S$195 per-session reference band, not a market average. Dividing proposed fees by scheduled weeks puts 13 of 20 courses within it and seven above. Clearly distinguish total fee from fee per session. Cupcake & Cake Pops (S$220 for one session) and Sushi & Sashimi (S$245 per session) need a clear explanation of their premium.

## The website must

1. Show each course's **total fee, session count and schedule** before booking.
2. Show the **hands-on techniques and take-home result** so visitors can judge value.
3. Make the **one-session entry courses and progression to longer courses** easy to find at both campuses.

Source: *Cook & Bake — Market Validation*, research checked 24 September 2026. Paid enrolment, fill rates and margins are **UNKNOWN**.
